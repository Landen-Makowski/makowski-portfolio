Why OSINT Interests Me

Open-source intelligence, or OSINT, involves collecting and analyzing information from publicly available sources. I enjoy the problem-solving side of OSINT, especially tasks involving online research, geolocation, digital traces, and verifying information across multiple sources.

OSINT Framework

OSINT Framework is a collection of open-source intelligence tools and resources organized by category. It is useful for quickly finding websites and services related to areas such as usernames, email addresses, domains, geolocation, and other types of online research.
